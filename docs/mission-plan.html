02

Crew assignments

Four workstreams, each with a clear owner and goal.

Yousef & Antonio

Geospatial & Planetary Data

Supply the real space and Earth terrain data behind the map and the physics.

  • Pull open datasets from NASA Earthdata, LRO/Diviner, PDS, CSA RADARSAT and ESA Copernicus.
  • Select 4–6 Moon/Mars landing sites by topography, geology, environment and resources.
  • Characterize matching Earth analogs, both known sites and new under-characterized ones.
  • Score planet ↔ Earth similarity; export elevation slices plus slope, wind, albedo and soil.
Eyad

Frontend, 3D Globes & Mission UI

Build the web app, the interactive globes and the mission control screens.

  • 3D Moon/Mars globe with landing zones and camera transitions to the Earth Analog Map.
  • Strategy & Analog Configurator for method, fuel, thrust, sensors, and gravity & atmosphere.
  • Post-Flight Engineering Report: telemetry, survivability and an Analog Fidelity Score.
  • Wire every screen into one smooth end-to-end flow.
Tim

Descent Simulation & Physics

Build the core landing mechanics, the physics engine and the pass/fail logic.

  • Physics over real elevation profiles: gravity, thrust, fuel burn, wind drag, terrain collision.
  • Strategies: autonomous hazard avoidance vs. manual thruster/gimbal control.
  • Sensor interference from dust and snow glare.
  • Touchdown thresholds decide success or crash; the telemetry goes to the report.
Furgan

Cutscenes, VFX & Game Visuals

Bring the simulation to life with cinematic sequences, polish and feedback.

  • Opening cutscene: the test lander enters the chosen Earth analog environment.
  • Failure ending: fireballs, debris particles and camera shake.
  • Success ending: clean touchdown, dust settling and beacon deployment.
  • Lander, exhaust, dust plumes, textures and sound, all driven by physics events.

03

Data flow

These integration contracts stay stable. When one changes, update every consumer in the same change.

Data → UI · Physics

Terrain data

Planetary sites, Earth analogs, similarity scores, elevation slices, and slope, wind, albedo and soil. Lives in data/ profiles and web/public/terrain/ packs.

Eyad → Tim · Furgan

Simulation input

Chosen site plus strategy: method, fuel, thrust, sensors, and gravity & atmosphere adjustments.

Tim → Furgan

Physics events

Thruster firing, dust kick-up, touchdown and crash, which trigger VFX, cutscenes and sound.

Tim → Eyad

Flight telemetry

Touchdown velocities, fuel left, slope tilt, outcome and survivability, which feed the report.

Flight rules
Working conventions for the whole crew.

    

      
Cite the data
Use real open data from NASA, CSA and ESA, and cite the source dataset for every number shown to users.

      
Contracts first
Build against the agreed contracts with sample data. Don't wait for final data.

      
Stay in your lane
Keep changes inside your own area, or coordinate through the shared contracts.
