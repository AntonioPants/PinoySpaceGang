# Textures for the view outside the ship's bridge

Illustration only: these dress the bodies seen through the bridge window. They are not
analysis data and do not show the selected sites. All three are public domain, resampled to
2048 × 1024 JPEG on 2026-11-14.

- `moon.jpg`: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (LRO/LROC WAC colour mosaic).
  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 (file `lroc_color_poles_2k.tif`)
- `mars.jpg`: Viking Orbiter MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic, NASA / JPL / USGS Astrogeology.
  https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/details/Mars/Viking/MDIM21/Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_global_232m/cub
  (downloaded as https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_1km.jpg)
- `earth.jpg`: NASA Blue Marble, land surface, shallow water and shaded topography
  (Reto Stöckli, NASA GSFC). https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57752
  (downloaded as https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg)
